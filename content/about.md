# Read Me

I build backend and data systems. Lately that means healthcare: referral pipelines
and the provider data underneath them, at a company called ExaCare.

## What I actually care about

Systems that don't need me watching them. The work I'm proudest of is the boring
kind: a reconciliation job that repairs its own data every week, an endpoint that
stopped setting off alarms 144 times a day, an on-call tool that cut the busywork
in half. If it needs a human babysitter, it isn't done.

## Why healthcare data?

Because the data is a genuine mess and the mess matters. Public provider records
disagree with each other, go stale, and move. Getting a dependable picture out of
that is a real problem, and people downstream actually feel it when you get it
right.

## The AI part

I brought Claude Code and the Superpowers workflow into ExaCare, then presented it
company-wide at Town Hall and Eng Week. I spend most of my day pairing with agents
now. It changed how fast the team ships.
